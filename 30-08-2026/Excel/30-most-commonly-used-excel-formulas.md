# Most Commonly Used Excel Formulas for Data Analysts

## Question
What are the most essential and frequently used Excel formulas in real-world data analysis?

---

## 1. Summary of Top Excel Formulas

| Category | Formulas | Primary Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lookup & Reference** | `XLOOKUP`, `INDEX`, `MATCH`, `OFFSET` | Joining and retrieving data across sheets. |
| **Aggregation & Math** | `SUMIFS`, `COUNTIFS`, `AVERAGEIFS` | Conditional metrics calculation. |
| **Text Manipulation** | `TEXTSPLIT`, `CONCAT`, `TRIM`, `LEFT`, `RIGHT`, `MID` | Cleansing messy strings and names. |
| **Date & Time** | `DATEDIF`, `EDATE`, `EOMONTH`, `YEAR`, `TODAY` | Aging analysis, billing cycles, cohort intervals. |
| **Logical & Decision**| `IF`, `IFS`, `SWITCH`, `AND`, `OR`, `IFERROR` | Conditional segmentation and error handling. |
| **Dynamic Arrays** | `UNIQUE`, `FILTER`, `SORT`, `SORTBY` | Live slicing and subset extraction. |
